Evaluated Paradigms
Nearest Neighbor
Greedy constructive heuristic visiting the closest unvisited node. Fast $O(N^2)$, but susceptible to trapped local loops.
2-Opt Local Search
Iterative improvement removing edge crossings by reversing tour segments until 2-optimal.
Genetic Algorithm (OX1)
Evolutionary metaheuristic balancing exploration and exploitation with tournament selection and order preservation.
Brute Force (Exact)
Exhaustive search evaluating $(N-1)!$ permutations. Mathematically guarantees true global optimum.
